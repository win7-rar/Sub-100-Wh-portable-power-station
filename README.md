# Sub-100-Wh-portable-power-station

I had made a portable power station that is of 99.9 Wh and packs a ton of necessary as well as unnecessary features

Features:
  1) 99.9 Wh, making it flight friendly
  2) Has 2x USB C PD 3.1 65 W fast chargers
  3) Has 2x USB A 24 W fast chargers
  4) Has a built in wireless charger
  5) Has a 200 W (continuous) inverter
  6) Has a variable lab bench power supply
  7) Has Linear resonant haptics (LRA), similar to the ones found in higher tier smartphones
  8) Has a 7 W built-in lamp
  9) Has various safety features such as overcurrent, short circuit, thermal and overdischarge protections
  10) Has a variable power limit selection
  11) Has an RGB LED bar with adjustable brightness levels
  12) Supports up to 100 W charging (MPPT tracking used) and also a USB 5 V charging.
  13) Supports ICSP programming
  14) Temperature controlled fan

CAD parts and 3D printing:

All parts were thoroughly tested for compatibility with each other in both the virtual as well as the physical environment.
Some parts require some amount of post processing for better compatibility.
M3 and M4 bolt and nuts were used to mount the enclosure parts and electronics to their housing.

Circuit:

The circuit was entirely random/unplanned (i.e: I did not build a virtual circuit but rather a direct physical circuit on the perf board whenever I got ideas.). However, the pinouts and the circuit planning are attached as images along with other miscellaneous sketches and plans I did on paper.

Firmware:

Despite facing many challenges, I took an initiative to not use any AI in my code/firmware. I had to do quite a bit of research, understand the root cause and fix it through hit and trial which is one of the best ways to learn.
